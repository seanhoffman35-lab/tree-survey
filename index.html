Species

Star a species to pin it to the top of the Record screen. Switch one off to hide its button while you are not looking for it. Hidden species keep their trees and still export. Tap a name to edit.

What to export

CSV for spreadsheets and Google My Maps. KML for Google Earth. GeoJSON for QGIS. The share sheet opens so you can AirDrop, message, mail, or save the file.

Backup

A backup holds everything: species, fields, and every tree. Restoring replaces what is on this phone.
